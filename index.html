    About the Maker

    A photograph is not a place I have been. It is a feeling I refused to leave behind.

    Zhunming Ma is a traveler who happens to carry a camera — a graduate student living between Ireland and the wider world, chasing the hour when ordinary streets turn golden.

    This archive is not a highlight reel. It is a diary of standing still: a mosque at dawn, a subway platform in Stockholm, the moment the sky over Iceland finally caught fire.
